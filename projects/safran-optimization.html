Safran Nacelles Cycle-Time Optimization

A constrained optimization study for allocating a theoretical cycle-time reduction across Gulfstream G700 nacelle assembly operations, with uncertainty tested through 5,000 Monte Carlo scenarios.

01 / Problem

A global target is not yet an operational improvement plan.

Reducing an assembly cycle requires deciding where improvement effort can be allocated while respecting operation-level feasibility, process dependencies, and quality constraints. Applying the same reduction everywhere would ignore the structure of the routing and create targets that some operations cannot absorb.

02 / Method

Translate an industrial objective into a constrained allocation model.

Routing data→Operation caps→Convex model→SLSQP→Feasible allocation
  1. Structure operation times, improvement opportunities, and process constraints in a reproducible Excel/Python workflow.
  2. Represent the total cycle-time objective as an allocation problem rather than a uniform percentage cut.
  3. Impose operation-level bounds so the solver cannot assign unrealistic reductions.
  4. Solve the constrained problem with SLSQP and check feasibility against the documented limits.
03 / Robustness

Test whether the allocation survives operational uncertainty.

A deterministic optimum can be fragile when durations vary. A 5,000-scenario Monte Carlo simulation perturbed the modeled conditions and evaluated how consistently the proposed allocation preserved the target and constraints.

Interpretation boundary

The simulation evaluates robustness within the modeled assumptions. It does not convert a theoretical allocation into a realized production gain without implementation and shop-floor measurement.

04 / Result

A defensible theoretical path from 148 to 146 hours.

The model identified a feasible distribution of improvement effort corresponding to a two-hour theoretical cycle-time reduction. The value of the work is not only the headline target: it is the traceable connection between each allocated reduction, its constraint, and its sensitivity to uncertainty.
