01 / Problem

Size a wing that is aerodynamically credible and structurally defensible.

The project connects multiple levels of fidelity. Analytical tools establish first-order geometry and loading, CFD checks the flow field and aerodynamic coefficients, and finite-element analysis tests the structural response under the resulting load cases.

02 / Method

A staged verification workflow.

Requirements→Wing sizing→Lifting-line→CFD→FEA
  1. Select the airfoil and define reference geometry and flight conditions.
  2. Use Prandtl lifting-line theory for spanwise loading and first-order performance.
  3. Build a STAR-CCM+ CFD case for pressure and flow-field analysis.
  4. Transfer representative loading to an Abaqus structural model.
  5. Compare results across methods and document modeling limits.
03 / CFD

Numerical flow analysis around the selected airfoil.

The CFD stage provides a more detailed view of the pressure distribution and wake than the analytical model, while remaining dependent on mesh quality, turbulence modeling, and boundary-condition choices.

04 / Structural analysis

Finite-element verification of the wing structure.

The finite-element model checks stress distribution and deformation under representative aerodynamic loading. Results are interpreted with attention to boundary-condition idealization and material assumptions.
